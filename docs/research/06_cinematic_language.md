## Shot sizes we use
- **Wide (WS):** whole group + environment; establishes geography (Shot 1, 6).
- **Medium (MS):** waist up / whole toddler at this size (Shot 5).
- **Medium close-up (MCU):** chest up, reads face acting (Shot 3).
- **Low angle / insert:** at puppy height (Shot 2).
- **POV:** what the character sees, camera tilted down (Shot 4).

## Rules
- **180° rule:** the line of action runs between Kanha and the puppy. Puppy always travels
  **left → right** on screen; Kanha looks screen-left/back toward it. Never cross the line.
- **Eyeline match:** in Shot 3 Kanha first looks back at eye level (nothing), then down. In
  Shot 4 the camera looks down at the puppy, and the puppy looks *up* toward camera-left-top
  where Kanha's face would be.
- **Cut on action:** cut 1→2 in the middle of the puppy's trot; cut 2→3 on the bump impact;
  cut 5→6 in the middle of the lift so the motion carries across the cut.
- **Reaction shots:** friends' cheer in Shot 6 is the payoff reaction.
- **Camera moves:** slow push-in (scale/translate ortho camera) on Shot 1; tiny drift on
  holds so frames are never dead; parallax layers move at different speeds during pans.
- **Readability without sound:** every story beat is a clear pose held long enough
  (≥ 8 f) to read: talking → bump → confused → sees puppy → aww → lift → cheer.
